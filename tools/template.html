How relevance is calculated

Objective: 35 points. Fuel phase: 20 when selected. Oxygen: 20. Pressure: 10. Source availability: 15. Planned ranges receive partial credit. Unknown conditions receive no credit.

Scores are normalized to 100. They rank research usefulness, not fire risk or safety. Gravity differences appear as evidence gaps.
